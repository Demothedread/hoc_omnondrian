Document → slides

Paste a document. Section headings become slides; a short capitalised line above a heading becomes its label; "Label: number" lines become big numbers; quotes, lists and images keep their roles; long sections continue onto another slide. The Outline shows the rule behind every block. On the site, this happens to whatever you write in the CMS, or import with npm run import.
